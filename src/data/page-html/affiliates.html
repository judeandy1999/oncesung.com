Clear expectations

Partner questions, answered.

How is commission calculated?

The proposed rate is 25% of qualifying song-package revenue actually retained by Oncesung, after discounts and refunds and excluding taxes. Gift cards are excluded unless your agreement says otherwise. An eligible $89 Signature order earns $22.25.

How will attribution work?

The proposed window is 30 days from an eligible affiliate-link click, using the last eligible affiliate click. Creator codes can support attribution only as agreed in your partner terms. Tracking and attribution must be operational before paid promotion begins.

When would I be paid?

Proposed payouts are monthly in USD, with a $50 minimum cleared balance and a 30-day hold after purchase. Smaller balances carry forward. Refunds and chargebacks reverse the associated commission. Payment method and timing will be confirmed in your agreement.

Can I receive a sample song or a sponsored placement?

Sample songs and paid integrations may be considered individually. Applying does not guarantee a sample, a sponsorship fee or acceptance. Any deliverables, content permissions and payment must be agreed before production.

How should I describe the music?

Describe it as personalized, AI-created music with human creative direction. Share the package’s actual revision and delivery terms. Do not promise a human-recorded performance, artist impersonation, commercial rights or results that have not been agreed.

What promotional rules apply?

Clearly disclose your affiliate relationship and any free sample or paid sponsorship. Use the platform’s paid-promotion tools where required. Get consent before sharing personal stories or recipient reactions. Self-referrals, spam, misleading claims and unapproved brand-keyword advertising are excluded.

Is this the same as Refer a Friend?

No. This program is for approved promotional partners earning monetary commission. The separate Refer a Friend page describes customer referral rewards.
