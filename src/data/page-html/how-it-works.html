How Oncesung works

A simple, guided process that turns your memories into an original song — written and produced just for them.

Start your song
From memories
to music ♡
  1. Step 1

    Choose the occasion

    Tell us who the song is for and what you're celebrating — birthday, anniversary, wedding, memorial or just because.

  2. Step 2

    Share their story

    Names, inside jokes, places, favourite memories. The more you share, the more personal the song.

  3. Step 3

    Pick the style & mood

    Pop, acoustic, country, cinematic and more. Choose a vibe — romantic, fun, emotional, uplifting.

  4. Step 4

    We write the lyrics

    A professional songwriter turns your story into original lyrics. Signature and Concierge include a lyrics review.

  5. Step 5

    We produce your song

    Studio-quality vocals and instruments. Your first version arrives in 48–72 hours.

  6. Step 6

    Review & refine

    Listen, request revisions, approve the version you love. Still not right? Our money-back guarantee covers you.

  7. Step 7

    Download & share

    Get MP3/WAV, lyrics and artwork. Share by link, email or social.
