Pricing questions

What's the difference between remakes and instrumental versions?

Remakes (Signature and Concierge) are new takes of your finished song, so you can choose your favorite. Instrumental versions (Concierge only) come earlier: you hear 3 different instrumentals first, pick one, and we pair your lyrics with it.

Can I review the lyrics before the song is made?

Yes, on Signature and Concierge. Essential goes straight from your questionnaire to the finished song, with 1 minor revision included.

How fast will I get my song?

Essential targets 48 hours, and Signature and Concierge 72 hours.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes, on every package. If you're still not happy after using your included revisions, ask within 14 days of receiving your final song and we'll refund the full price. How it works

Which file formats do I get?

Essential includes MP3. Signature and Concierge add WAV for higher quality.
